Robotic Manipulator

Overview

An open-source 5 degrees of freedom bimanual robotic arm setup designed as a low-cost platform to experiment with embodied AI.

Setup of the robotic arm.
Hardware

Built with 6x ST3215 serial bus servos on a 20×20 aluminum extrusion frame, with PLA/PETG printed joints (STLs in repo).
